# Institution Rules Registry — Coverage and Limits

Status as of 2026-10-23. This document describes what the institution-level
(복무기관별) registry in `packages/rules/institutions/registry.json` contains,
how it was researched, and where it is empty. The tables below are generated
from the registry and the research log; the prose is maintained by hand.

## What the registry is for

The nationwide compensation bundle (`packages/rules/compensation/2026.json`)
fixes base pay, the 2026 meal **minimum** (9,000원) and the transport fare
basis. Everything an individual payer decides on top of that is
institution-specific:

- the meal amount it actually pays (≥ the minimum);
- the transport amount or method;
- whether 휴가·결근·교육 days earn meal and transport money;
- half-day leave, late arrival and early leave;
- rounding (단수처리);
- pay day;
- work-day and holiday handling.

The registry stores those facts one at a time, each with its source.
`lookupInstitutionRules(name, { year })` (import from `@super-gongik/rules/institutions`; it is kept out of the package root so the iOS core bundle does not carry the registry) returns them, or
`UNKNOWN_INSTITUTION`, or `UNKNOWN` for any field nobody has documented.

## Rules the data follows

1. **No invented values.** A field is `KNOWN` only when a retrieved official
   document states it. Anything else is `{ "status": "UNKNOWN" }`. Nothing is
   inferred from a neighbouring institution, a national average, last year's
   value or a blog.
2. **Every fact carries evidence:** source id, page/section locator and a
   verbatim quote. `packages/rules/tests/institutions.test.ts` checks that each
   quote occurs in the stored extract (whitespace ignored), that every amount
   is written in its quote, that every stored file matches its SHA-256 and
   `SHA256SUMS`, and that every source is on an official domain.
3. **Payment rule vs budget unit price.** `valueKind: "BUDGET_UNIT_PRICE"`
   marks a 예산편성 기준단가: the number a payer budgets with, not proof of
   what is paid. The lookup adds a warning for it.
4. **Confidence is derived, not chosen.** `HIGH` means a rule, ordinance,
   institution guideline or payment approval (지급 결재문서) states the fact.
   `MEDIUM` means a budget guideline, an official explanatory web page or an
   official Q&A. The schema rejects any other combination.
5. **Exact-name matching only.** Names are normalised (NFKC, no spaces or
   punctuation) and matched against the official name and listed aliases. A
   partial match is returned as a suggestion and never applied: "경기도" must
   not pick up 경기도교육청's rules. Short district names that exist in more
   than one city (중구, 동구, 서구, 남구, 북구, 강서구 …) are never aliases.
6. **Year matters.** Each fact has `effectiveYear`. Asking for 2026 when only a
   2024 or 2025 fact exists returns the fact with `yearMatches: false` and a
   warning.
7. **The registry does not change the calculator.** `evaluateMonthlyCompensation`
   still takes meal and fare as user input or the official minimum. A UI may
   offer a registry value for the user to confirm. That value is still user
   input, and a 2026 meal amount below 9,000원 is still refused.

## How it was researched

- **When:** retrieved 2026-10-21 to 2026-10-23.
- **Priority:** 시도교육청 (예산편성 기본지침, 복무관리 매뉴얼), then 광역·기초
  지자체 (복무관리 계획, 지급 결재문서, 예산 기준), then 중앙행정기관 훈령 and
  공공기관 internal rules on law.go.kr.
- **Portals:** 서울 정보소통광장 결재문서 (opengov.seoul.go.kr), 정보공개포털 원문정보
  (open.go.kr), 교육청 재정·예산 boards and law.go.kr 행정규칙.
- **Text extraction:** pypdf for PDF; the HWPX section XML for HWPX; kordoc or an
  OLE reader for HWP; kordoc's local OCR for scanned PDFs. Hand-typed
  transcriptions are not accepted: a scanned 구미시 document typed by hand was
  dropped for that reason.
- **Storage:** originals up to 600 KB are stored next to their extract. Larger
  originals are pinned by SHA-256 and size only, and the extract keeps the
  cited pages.

User request (2026-10-23): survey every institution in 제주 that uses 사회복무요원. Every institution the research looked at is listed here, including the ones with no public evidence.

**How many there are.** 병무청's 「사회복무요원 복무기관」 open API (data.go.kr 3066757, fetched 2026-10-23 with `scripts/fetch-mma-service-institutions.cjs`) lists **380** 복무기관 under **96** 대표기관 in the 제주 office. Every one of the 96 대표기관 is cross-checked against the registry and this survey in the 제주 section of `docs/INSTITUTION_ROSTER_CENSUS.md`. (An older aggregate, 「기관별 청별 현황」 data.go.kr 15119985 as of 2025-07-31, counted 16 국가기관, 9 자치단체, 9 공공단체 and 182 복지시설.) The tables below are the research log, grouped by who runs the institution.

**Result.** 10 FOUND, 7 covered by a parent agency's rule, 2 paid inside a parent body's payment, 112 NOT_FOUND (searched, no public official evidence), 2 BLOCKED.

- `FOUND`: the institution has its own record in the registry (id shown).
- `COVERED_BY_PARENT`: the parent agency's rule (already in the registry) names this office in its scope clause; no separate record.
- `PAID_UNDER_PARENT`: the parent body's payment approvals include this unit's agents, but no rule names it; no separate record.
- `NOT_FOUND`: searched; nothing official states its payment rules. Its agents fall back to the nationwide 병무청 rules and to what the user enters.

## Limits

- **Coverage is far from complete.** Korea has about 226 시군구, 17 시도,
  17 시도교육청 and hundreds of public bodies. Many internal 복무관리 계획 are not
  public, or are public with amounts masked (Seoul 결재문서 often mask amounts,
  and 지급조서 attachments are 비공개). An unknown here means "no public
  official document found", not "the institution has no rule".
- **Older documents.** Some sources predate 2026: 국가인권위원회 2019, five 시군구 payment documents from 2024 (광주 서구, 성주군, 포항시, 목포시, 금천구, plus 광명시 in part),
  조달청 2015, 서울시설공단 2016, 강남구 2022, 대전교육청 2024 and 서울교통공사 2024. Their facts are stored with that year, so a 2026 lookup flags them.
- **Single payment instances.** A payment approval for one month (e.g. 강동구
  2025-09-10, 미래한강본부 2026-10-15) is evidence of practice, not a standing
  rule. Its value text says so.
- **Second-hand figures are excluded.** One institution's document listing
  other institutions' amounts (구미시's survey of other 경북 시군) is not
  recorded for those other institutions.
- **Rounding.** No public institution document found states its own 단수처리.
  The app keeps using only the explicit policies in
  `docs/PRORATION_EVIDENCE_2026.md`.
- **Schools.** A school's 사회복무요원 is usually paid from its 학교회계, which
  follows the 교육청's 학교회계 예산편성 기본지침. The lookup does not map a
  school name to its 교육청. The UI should ask which 교육청 the school belongs
  to.

## Finding to hand back to the compensation policy

The 병무청's own page "보수 및 권익보장" (mma.go.kr) states, verbatim,
"교통비,급식비 실제 근무일에 대해 실비 지급 휴가, 결근 등 비근무일 미지급".
경기도교육청's 2025 복무관리 매뉴얼 and 인천광역시교육청's 2026 지침 say the same.
`docs/COMPENSATION_POLICY_2026.md` currently classifies full-day leave as
AMBIGUOUS and leaves the decision to the user. That page is an official
explanatory page, not a regulation (confidence MEDIUM here). Whether it should
turn the leave-day default into "not paid" is a policy decision for the
compensation bundle owner and is **not** made in this change.

## Re-running and extending

- **Adding an institution:**
  1. Store the original, or its cited pages, under
     `docs/sources/institutions/<source-id>/`.
  2. Add the source and the fact to `registry.json`.
  3. Regenerate `SHA256SUMS`.
  4. Run `pnpm --filter @super-gongik/rules test`.
- **Planned user reports:** facts users report about their own institution are
  a separate track. They must not be written into this registry as official
  evidence. A future `sourceType` for them would have to stay distinguishable
  and below `MEDIUM`.
